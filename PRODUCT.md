# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Music producers, songwriters, publishers, and independent catalog owners who need clear answers about registrations, ownership, publishing administration, royalty collection, unauthorized uses, or advance readiness. Visitors may arrive uncertain about what is missing from their catalog and need a credible specialist who can turn fragmented rights data into an actionable next step.

## Product Purpose

Keyman presents music metadata and rights consulting services, establishes professional credibility, and converts qualified visitors into scheduled consultations. A secondary conversion path offers a no-cost Rights Gap Analysis for five song titles. Success means a visitor can understand the services, trust the experience behind them, book through Google Calendar, or submit the appropriate form without confusion.

## Brand Personality

Precise, assured, and discreet. The experience should feel like a specialist rights and analytics consultancy, with enough music-industry character to remain distinctive.

## Anti-references

Avoid the visual language of a recording studio, artist fan page, record label launch, nightlife promotion, generic SaaS dashboard, or luxury publishing firm. Do not use dark neon studio imagery, equalizer gimmicks, gold-on-black prestige styling, stock photos of performers, or vague claims unsupported by the supplied credentials.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with clarity: explain complex rights work in direct language.
2. Make consultation booking the clearest action on every relevant screen.
3. Demonstrate expertise through specific services, experience, and process rather than inflated claims.
4. Keep the free catalog review useful and low-friction without competing with the primary booking path.
5. Treat submitted catalog details as professional, sensitive information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, form instructions and errors, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, zoom, and responsive layouts from small phones through wide desktop screens.
